# Changelog

## 0.1.5 (21 Nov 2026)

- The report ends with a pointer to Build Brief, and Build Brief's "Belief Check starter" is recognised as a starting point. README: "Works with Build Brief".

## 0.1.4 (19 Nov 2026)

After round 3 (translators report rated "Easy to use", dental "Usable with effort"):
- Each verdict says whether the "wrong if..." was met, and why.
- Options list every belief they depend on, with its verdict, so none looks safer than the evidence allows.
- Evidence must show why it counts; counts say who they cover; no nudging words.
- "How to read this" explains "How sure" and "one person's account".
- "How this was done" as short bullets, no process notes or file paths.
- Summary limits start with the belief that sinks the idea.

## 0.1.3 (19 Nov 2026)

After an independent reader test and process audit of round 2 (all 15 spot-checked quotes were real):
- A verdict can't rest on something that wasn't collected or recorded. Interview notes: each person marked yes, no or not recorded.
- "Wrong if..." must test the belief itself, not a stand-in. Use the units sources give; simple unit changes allowed and shown.
- Searches against count only if a result page was opened. Quotes must contain the claimed fact. Report counts come from a search log.
- Report: "How to read this" key after At a glance; short labels per line, read date once; plain "why" line instead of rule numbers; no instruction words; limits say what they changed; "How this was done" at the end of part 6.
- Beliefs numbered in ranked order. "Test further" option goes last. Gains are facts, not advice. "Desk only" is now "online only".

## 0.1.2 (19 Nov 2026)

After a second round of four blind runs (all confirmed before searching; reports about half as long):
- Tie-break for the extra checks: value if right, then least sure, then earlier ID.
- New source type: association or professional body.
- Counting what you found can decide a verdict; only new figures you work out are estimates.
- The second check makes a verdict Mixed only when its evidence meets the bar on its own.

## 0.1.1 (19 Nov 2026)

After four blind test runs:
- No web search before the person confirms the beliefs, not even to pick numbers. All four runs searched early.
- "Wrong if..." must describe the world, never the search ("fewer than 2 sources show..." is not allowed). Finding nothing is "Not enough evidence".
- Interview thresholds as shares ("fewer than half"), not fixed counts.
- Beliefs written as positive sentences.
- New source type: news or trade report. Estimates the AI works out are labelled as its own arithmetic.
- Sources list only the sources cited, plus a count of the rest.
- Second check runs after people research is analysed or declined.

Readability rules for the report: "At a glance" block, verdict first, at most 3 points for and against, short quotes, one table.
